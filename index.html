Building and Evaluating a Digital Twin for Severe Traumatic Brain Injury: Predicting Recovery Milestones and Refining Prognosis Through Sequential Updating

Negar Haghpanahi, Ganapati Bhat, Amy Kemp, Frank Jackson · Washington State University and St. Luke’s Medical Rehabilitation Center

ReferencesContact

The study in brief

We predicted three recovery milestones after moderate-to-severe traumatic brain injury from the TBI Model Systems National Database: time to follow commands (TFC), post-traumatic amnesia (PTA) duration, and the Glasgow Outcome Scale–Extended (GOS-E). We then tested whether a prediction improves once an earlier milestone is known.

7.7 vs. 15.2 dPTA mean absolute error, best model vs. naïve baseline
AUC 0.79GOS-E poor vs. favorable outcome (baseline 0.50)
−0.28 dPTA error once actual TFC is known: 7.73 → 7.45 d (95% CI 0.19–0.37); improved in 8 of 10 models
1.18 · 1.69Follow-up GOS-E and DRS mean absolute error (RL model, held-out test); 21% and 9% lower than carry-forward when outcomes changed
